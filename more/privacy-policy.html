Introduction

Welcome to Nomekop Bequest ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and share your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information we collect through our platform, website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Important: By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

This policy applies to all users of our Services, including players, NFT collectors, PKC token holders, and visitors to our website.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, password (encrypted), and profile information
  • Wallet Information: Your blockchain wallet address (public key)
  • Communication Data: Messages, support tickets, and feedback you send to us
  • Transaction History: Records of your purchases, trades, and in-game activities
  • Payment Information: Processed by third-party payment providers; we do not store full credit card numbers

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Services, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device ID
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on platform, gameplay statistics
  • Location Data: General geographic location based on IP address
  • Cookies and Tracking: See Section 5 for detailed information

Blockchain Data

Because Nomekop Bequest operates on blockchain technology, certain information is publicly visible on the blockchain, including:

  • Your wallet address
  • NFT ownership and transaction history
  • PKC token transactions and balances
  • Smart contract interactions

Note: Blockchain transactions are permanent and publicly accessible. We cannot delete or modify information recorded on the blockchain.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Providing and Improving Our Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process transactions and marketplace activities
  • Enable gameplay features and matchmaking
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve game balance, features, and user experience

Security and Fraud Prevention

  • Detect and prevent fraud, cheating, and unauthorized activities
  • Monitor for security threats and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Verify your identity and protect your account

Communication

  • Send important updates about your account and our Services
  • Notify you about platform changes, new features, and events
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Respond to your questions and support requests

Legal Compliance

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Respond to legal requests and prevent illegal activities
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud)
  • Payment processors (Stripe, cryptocurrency payment gateways)
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics)
  • Customer support platforms
  • Email service providers

Public Blockchain

Your wallet address and all blockchain transactions are publicly visible on the blockchain and can be viewed by anyone using blockchain explorers.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • Investigate fraud or security issues
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you of any such change.

With Your Consent

We may share your information for purposes not described in this policy with your explicit consent.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities. For detailed information, please see our Cookie Policy.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Services
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect your ability to use some features of our Services.

Blockchain and Public Information

Nomekop Bequest operates on public blockchain networks. This means that certain information is permanently recorded and publicly accessible:

  • Your wallet address is visible to all blockchain participants
  • All NFT transactions and ownership records are public
  • PKC token transactions are visible on blockchain explorers
  • Smart contract interactions are publicly recorded

Important: We cannot control or delete information recorded on the blockchain. Blockchain transactions are permanent and irreversible. Please be mindful of the information you choose to associate with your wallet address.

PKC Token Transactions

When you engage in transactions using PKC tokens, the following information is recorded on the blockchain:

  • Transaction amount and timestamp
  • Sender and recipient wallet addresses
  • Transaction fees (gas costs)
  • Smart contract function called

We may use this on-chain data to:

  • Display your transaction history within the platform
  • Verify ownership of NFTs and tokens
  • Prevent fraud and ensure compliance with our Terms
  • Analyze platform usage and economics

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

Technical Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Firewall protection and intrusion detection systems
  • Access controls and least-privilege principles

Organizational Measures

  • Employee training on data protection
  • Strict data access policies
  • Regular security awareness programs
  • Incident response procedures

Your Responsibilities

While we implement strong security measures, you also have responsibilities:

  • Keep your password secure and confidential
  • Protect your wallet private keys (we never ask for these)
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your account
  • Report suspicious activities immediately
  • Use secure internet connections

Warning: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Portability

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to receive it in a portable format.

Correction

You can update your account information at any time through your account settings. You may also request correction of inaccurate information.

Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions:

  • Information required for legal compliance
  • Information necessary to resolve disputes
  • Blockchain data (which cannot be deleted)

Opt-Out of Marketing

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking "unsubscribe" in our emails
  • Adjusting your account notification settings
  • Contacting us at privacy@nomekop.com

Do Not Track

Some browsers have "Do Not Track" features. Currently, we do not respond to these signals, but you can control cookies through your browser settings.

Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@nomekop.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Nomekop Bequest is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@nomekop.com. We will delete such information promptly.

Important: Users must be at least 18 years old to engage in cryptocurrency transactions or purchase NFTs. Users between 13-18 may use the platform with parental consent but cannot make purchases.

International Data Transfers

Nomekop Bequest operates globally and may transfer your information to countries other than your own. These countries may have different data protection laws.

Data Transfer Mechanisms

When we transfer data internationally, we use appropriate safeguards:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by regulatory authorities
  • Adequacy decisions by relevant data protection authorities
  • Your explicit consent

EU and UK Users

For users in the European Union and United Kingdom, we comply with GDPR requirements for international data transfers.

California Users

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). See Section 9 for information about your rights.
