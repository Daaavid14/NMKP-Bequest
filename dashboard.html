❓ Support

📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Create an account, choose your starter Nomekop, and begin exploring the world! Complete daily quests to earn PKC and grow your collection.

Navigate to your PKC Wallet and click "Buy PKC". You can purchase tokens using supported payment methods including credit card, crypto, or bank transfer.

Choose up to 3 Nomekops for your team, then select Quick Match or Ranked Match. Battles are turn-based — choose moves wisely using type advantages!

Go to the Marketplace, click "Sell NFT", select the Nomekop you want to sell, set a price in PKC, and confirm. Your listing will be visible to all traders.

Staking locks your PKC for a period (30, 90, or 180 days) to earn interest. The longer you stake, the higher your APY reward. You can track active stakes in your Wallet.
